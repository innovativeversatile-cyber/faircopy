How it works

  1. Add the pages. Use the camera or pick from your gallery. A letter, a form, an ID, a receipt — anything flat.
  2. Tidy them if you need to. Rotate, crop, and choose a look. Paper clears a yellow cast. Ink is for text.
  3. Make the PDF. The file is written on this device and saved here. We never receive it. There is no copy in an account, because there is no account.

What this is, and is not

This is a copying tool. It is not a notary, a government office, or a place to store records. Do not use it to alter identity papers in order to deceive anyone.

Three pages are free — enough for a letter, a form, or a card. A longer packet can be split into two PDFs, or unlocked with a pass.
